Day 18: Performance Optimization (Debounce & Throttle)

High-frequency events like input, resize, and scroll can fire hundreds of times per second, freezing the UI. Rate-limiting keeps performance buttery smooth.

1. Debouncing (Auto-Complete & Search Inputs)

Concept: "Wait until the user pauses for X ms before firing." Every new keystroke resets the countdown timer.
